HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY - DEMO

Policy Name: Silver Health Plan

Hospitalization:
The policy covers eligible hospitalization expenses up to PKR 500,000 per policy year.

Room Charges:
Room charges are covered up to PKR 5,000 per day.

Waiting Period:
A waiting period of 30 days applies to general illnesses. Accidental hospitalization is exempt from this waiting period.

Pre-existing Conditions:
Pre-existing medical conditions are not covered during the first 24 months of the policy.

Exclusions:
Cosmetic surgery, experimental treatments and non-prescribed medicines are excluded.

Claims:
The policyholder must submit original hospital bills and claim documents within 30 days of discharge.

Emergency:
Emergency hospitalization requires notification to the insurer within 24 hours.
